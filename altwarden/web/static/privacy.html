AltWarden

Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

AltWarden checks people who join Discord servers. This page says exactly what it reads, what it keeps, and for how long. The source code is public, so you can confirm every line here.

The short version

  • Your IP address, email, cookie and browser fingerprint are never stored in readable form.
  • Your Discord sign-in is revoked seconds after it's used.
  • The verification page has no ads, analytics or other outside scripts.
  • The hashes in one server can't be connected to the hashes in another. Your Discord ID is stored as it is, so staff can see who verified.
  • You can see and delete your data yourself at My data.

What is read during verification

Your email addressOnly requested if the server turned on the throwaway email check. The domain is compared with a list, then the address is dropped. It's never written down.
IP addressCompared in memory with lists of VPNs, data centers and countries. A keyed hash is stored for alt matching. The address isn't stored.
Browser and device detailsCompared to find spoofing, automation and virtual machines. Only the result is stored, as labels such as "private window". A browser identifier and a device value are stored as keyed hashes.
Operating system and browser familyStored as keyed hashes, so staff can see whether two linked accounts also share a system and browser. They never count toward a match.
A random cookieSet to recognize the same browser. Stored as a keyed hash.

What a keyed hash means

A keyed hash is a one-way scramble made with a secret key. AltWarden can tell whether two values match, but the hash can't be turned back into the address or fingerprint without that key. Every server has its own derived key, so the same person produces unrelated hashes in two servers. The key isn't stored in the database, so a copy of the database alone reveals nothing.

A timer deletes expired records automatically. If a server removes AltWarden, everything held for that server is deleted after 30 days, and /uninstall deletes it at once.

None of them can be read by scripts, and none is shared with anyone. The dashboard also remembers its theme and accent color in your browser, which never leaves your device.

Who can see what

A server's staff see your Discord name, your attempts, which check stopped you, and which account you were linked to and how strongly. They never see an IP address, email, cookie or fingerprint, because AltWarden doesn't have them in readable form. Staff never see your country.

Outside services

Member pages load nothing from outside this site, apart from your Discord profile picture and the server's icon, which come from Discord's image service. AltWarden shares nothing with advertisers or data brokers.

One optional exception: a server can turn on an outside VPN lookup. If it does, your IP address is sent to proxycheck.io during verification. It's off unless a server chooses it.

Your choices

Sign in at My data to see everything held about you and delete it, for one server or all of them, with no request to anyone. Deleting your records also removes your verified role, because there's nothing left to support it, and you can verify again whenever you like.

Deleting also removes staff records about you, such as a note that staff verified you, takes your name off anything you did as staff, and removes your ID from other people's records.

Two exceptions. If you're banned from a server, your hashes for that server stay until the ban ends or they expire, and they go at once when it ends. Otherwise a banned account could erase the only thing that catches its next account. Your name, picture and history are still deleted.

If a server limits attempts, makes you wait between them, or gives you a time limit to verify, your Discord ID stays with that count and timer, with no name or picture, until the attempt history setting removes it, counted from your last failed attempt. Otherwise deleting your data would reset the limit.

You can decline by not verifying. Each server decides whether it offers another way in.

Automated decisions

Each server chooses its rules, and AltWarden applies them automatically. If you think a result is wrong, use the Appeal button, and a person on that server's staff will review it.

Children

AltWarden is for people old enough to use Discord in their country.

Contact

For a decision about a server, talk to its staff. For anything about AltWarden itself, open an issue on GitHub. Don't post personal details there.
